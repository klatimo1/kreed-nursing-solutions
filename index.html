    @kimsnursinggems Prompt

    When caring for a client with acute hypoxia, what is the FIRST nursing action before calling the provider?

    KReed Nursing Solutions LLC Click card for Clinical Gem →
    The Nursing Gem Tap to flip back

    Raise the Head of the Bed (High-Fowler's Position)!

    Always assess and intervene physically first! Elevating the HOB maximizes chest expansion and lung ventilation immediately—before applying oxygen or notifying the healthcare provider.

    Memory Hook: "Position before oxygen, assess before calling!"
